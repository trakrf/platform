# TrakRF demo laptop — setup card

**In the bag:** the laptop, its charger (UGREEN 100W), the Wi-Fi stick, and
for the doorway reader the PoE switch and its cables.

## Before the demo

1. **Plug everything into the charger first**: the Wi-Fi stick, then the
   charger into the wall. Adding something later makes the charger restart
   all its ports for a moment.
2. **Plugging in power turns the laptop on.** Wait about two minutes. The
   TrakRF app opens on the screen by itself.
3. **Getting online somewhere new:** turn on your phone's hotspot. It must be
   named **`trakrf-uplink`**, password **`__________`** (set it on the phone
   once). The laptop joins it on its own.
4. **Doorway reader:** reader → PoE switch → the **laptop's Ethernet port**.
   That port is for the reader only. Never plug it into the venue's network.

## During the demo

- Badges, gateways and the alarm plug join the laptop's own Wi-Fi, **TrakRF**,
  automatically.
- Handheld reader (CS108): connect it from the app on this laptop's screen.
- Closing the lid while plugged in is fine. The demo keeps running.

## Packing up

- **Press the power button once.** The laptop shuts down cleanly. Wait for the
  screen to go dark before unplugging.
- Closing the lid while unplugged also shuts it down cleanly.

## If something is wrong

Make sure the laptop is online (step 3) and message Mike. As long as it's
online, everything else can be fixed remotely.
